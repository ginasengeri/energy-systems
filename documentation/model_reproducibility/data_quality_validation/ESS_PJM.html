PJM Energy Storage System Model

Data quality & validation notes for the energy storage system (ESS) optimization model, PJM grid.

This page documents data quality details for the PJM ESS optimization model, queried via the (grid=pjm, dataset=ess-model).

Model notes

A missing row in the regulation-details dataset resulted in NaN values in the final merged ess-model dataset, at 2025-08-22 03:00:00 UTC (2025-08-21 23:00:00 local time). This data is missing from PJM's (public database) and was excluded from the model's input data.

The model simulates a battery's operations from July 1st to October 1st, prior to the unifying of the RegA and RegD in PJM's market. This means mileage ratios represent the ratio between RegD and RegA, per the market rules prior to the change on October 1st.

RMCCPs and RMPCPs are used to determine prices for committed regulation. For regulation service revenues, only the RegA product is considered due to data-permission limitations. The regulation signal uses normalized sample data for one day, October 3, 2025, repeated across hours for the models period Q3 2025. This is due to data-accessibility limitations.
